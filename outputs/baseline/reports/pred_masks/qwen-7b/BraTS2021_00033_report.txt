1. Findings: The MRI slice shows a heterogeneous mass in the left temporal lobe. The enhancing tumor (ET) is predominantly located in the periphery, with a central area of tumor core/necrosis (TC) and peritumoral edema (WT/ED) surrounding it. The size of the enhancing tumor appears to be approximately 3 cm in diameter.

2. Assessment: The tumor appears to be WHO CNS5 Grade III (anaplastic astrocytoma).

3. Recommendations: Further imaging with contrast-enhanced MRI and possibly a biopsy to confirm the diagnosis and determine the grade. Consider referral to a neuro-oncologist for discussion of treatment options, which may include surgery, radiation therapy, and chemotherapy.
