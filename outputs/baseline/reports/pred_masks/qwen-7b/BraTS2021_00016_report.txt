The tumor core/necrosis (TC) is located within the enhancing tumor, appearing as a non-enhancing area. 3. Recommendations: Further imaging with contrast-enhanced MRI and possibly a biopsy to confirm the diagnosis and determine the grade. Consider referral to a neuro-oncologist for treatment planning.
