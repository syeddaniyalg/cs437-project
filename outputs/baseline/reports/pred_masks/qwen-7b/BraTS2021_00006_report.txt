   - Whole Tumor/Peritumoral Edema (WT/ED): Mild peritumoral edema is noted surrounding the enhancing tumor, with no significant mass effect or midline shift observed.

2. Assessment: 
   - WHO CNS5 Grade: Grade II (Astrocytoma)

3. Recommendations:

   - Consider adjuvant therapy based on the grade and extent of the tumor.
