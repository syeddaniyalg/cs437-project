## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 156.69 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with a high peritumoral Flair ratio of 1.9927 and an edema-to-T2-weighted ratio of 3.0322, indicating significant surrounding edema. The tumor has a multifocal pattern of growth, involving the cortical region and extending to the midline. Notably, the lesion shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, which warrants CTV expansion in radiation planning.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's multifocal pattern and cortical involvement suggest that surgical resection may be challenging.
* Focality: Given the lesion's size and location, eloquent cortex risks are present. Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* CTV expansion: Due to ependymal contact and proximity, CTV should be expanded to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
* Cortical involvement: Truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) to avoid unnecessary radiation exposure (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* SRS contraindication: The lesion's size (> 30mm) contraindicates SRS as primary modality (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
