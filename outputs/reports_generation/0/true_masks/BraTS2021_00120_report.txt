## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 73.61 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with a high peritumoral Flair ratio of 1.9241 and an edema-to-T2-weighted ratio of 0.617, indicating significant surrounding edema. The tumor has a moderate degree of necrosis, accounting for approximately 18.69% of its volume. Notably, the lesion shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, which warrants CTV expansion in radiation planning.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS). The tumor's location and features also raise the prior probability of IDH-mutant status.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment is crucial due to the lesion's size and multifocal nature.
* Focality is a concern, as the tumor involves eloquent cortex in the dominant hemisphere (LEFT). Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* CTV expansion is required to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
* Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
