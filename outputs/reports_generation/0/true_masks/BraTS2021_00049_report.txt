The tumor shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, warranting CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)

This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).
