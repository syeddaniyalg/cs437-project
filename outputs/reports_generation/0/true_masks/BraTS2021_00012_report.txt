## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 112 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits ring-enhancing pattern with significant necrosis, accounting for 65% of its volume. The ependymal contact and ventricular proximity suggest a high likelihood of infiltration into the surrounding neural structures. Additionally, cortical involvement is evident, necessitating careful consideration during surgical planning.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS). The IDH-mutant prior probability is raised due to the frontal predominance without bilateral or midline crossing (IDH-MUTANT-PRIOR).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability: The lesion's size and location necessitate a thorough evaluation of eloquent cortex risks.
* Focality: Unifocal tumor, but cortical involvement requires careful planning to preserve speech.
* Eloquent cortex risk: Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated due to the tumor's presence in the dominant hemisphere (ELOQUENT-CORTEX-LEFT).

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* CTV expansion: Include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
* Cortical involvement: Truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull, without margin into bone/air (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
