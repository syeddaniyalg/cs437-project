### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe, with heterogeneous enhancement and significant necrosis (19.42%). The lesion exhibits ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, measuring approximately 5mm from the ventricular wall. Cortical involvement is evident, with satellites present. The tumor's shape is irregular, and its volume is large, with a maximum 3D diameter of 77.78mm.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (GBM) in the IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation according to WHO-GBM-Necrosis.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The lesion's multifocal nature and cortical involvement may require a more extensive resection.
* Focality: Given the presence of satellites, eloquent cortex risks should be considered during surgical planning.
* Cortical truncation: Due to cortical involvement, it is recommended to truncate the CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) to avoid unnecessary radiation exposure.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG-Guideline: CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact. (RTOG-CTV-Ependymal)
* RTOG-Guideline: Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air). (RTOG-CTV-Cortical)
* Contraindication: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm. (RTOG-SRS-Exclusion)
