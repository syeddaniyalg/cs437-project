Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 IDH-wildtype glioblastoma, as supported by the presence of necrosis. The categorical profile indicates an irregular shape with heterogeneous enhancement, consistent with this diagnosis.

* RANO warnings:

* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
