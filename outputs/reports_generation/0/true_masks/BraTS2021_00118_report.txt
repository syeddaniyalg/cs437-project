### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 144.6 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with a high peritumoral Flair ratio of 1.8493 and an edema-to-T2-weighted ratio of 1.3797, indicating significant surrounding edema. The tumor has a moderate degree of heterogeneity (0.6813) and is characterized by the presence of necrosis, which accounts for approximately 11% of its volume. Notably, the lesion shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, with a distance of only 5 mm from the ventricular wall.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (GBM) in the IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation according to WHO criteria (WHO-GBM-Necrosis). The lesion's irregular shape and enhancement pattern also align with this diagnosis.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

* Resectability assessment: Given the tumor's size and location, resectability is uncertain.
* Focality: The lesion is multicentric, which may impact surgical planning.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Due to the tumor's presence in the dominant hemisphere, awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-Ependymal).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-Cortical).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to the presence of necrosis.
* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-Exclusion).
