### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 89.94 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with a high peritumoral Flair ratio of 1.7108 and an edema-to-T2-weighted ratio of 1.3825, indicating significant surrounding edema. The tumor has cortical involvement and is situated near the midline, with a ventricular proximity of 5 mm. Notably, the lesion shows ependymal contact, which warrants CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's size and location suggest that resection may be feasible, but careful planning is necessary to avoid eloquent cortex risks.
* Focality: The lesion is unifocal, which simplifies surgical planning.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**

* **RTOG Radiation Contouring Modifications:**

	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* **RANO Warnings:**
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to significant surrounding edema and cortical involvement.
* **SRS Contraindication:** SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).

**Additional Considerations:**
The tumor's location in the dominant hemisphere triggers a warning for eloquent cortex risks, indicating the need for awake craniotomy mapping to preserve speech.
