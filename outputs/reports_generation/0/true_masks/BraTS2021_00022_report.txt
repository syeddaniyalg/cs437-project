## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 91mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with a high peritumoral Flair ratio of 1.9212 and an edema-to-Tc ratio of 0.7293, indicating significant surrounding edema. The tumor has ependymal contact and is situated 5mm from the ventricular system. Cortical involvement is evident, and the lesion does not exhibit midline crossing or bilateral features.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS). The frontal lobe predominance without bilateral or midline crossing raises the probability of an IDH-mutant glioma (IDH-MUTANT-PRIOR).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**

* Resectability assessment: Given the tumor's size and location, a thorough surgical planning is necessary to ensure optimal resection.
* Focality: The lesion is unifocal, which supports a single surgical approach.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Due to the dominant hemisphere involvement, awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**

* CTV expansion: Expand the clinical target volume (CTV) to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
* Cortical involvement: Truncate the CTV margin at the inner table of the skull, without extending into bone or air (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* SRS contraindication: Due to the tumor's size (> 30mm), stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) as primary modality is contraindicated (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
