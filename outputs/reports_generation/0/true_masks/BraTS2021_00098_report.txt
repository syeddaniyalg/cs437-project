### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic and topographical findings, as well as the triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is WHO Grade 4 Glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, ependymal contact, and cortical involvement.

* Molecular Prior: The frontal predominance without bilateral/midline crossing raises the probability of IDH-mutant glioblastoma.
* Contraindication: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
