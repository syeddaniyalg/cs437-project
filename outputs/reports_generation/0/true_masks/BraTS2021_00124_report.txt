## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe, measuring approximately 117mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits a ring-enhancing pattern on T1ce-weighted imaging, with significant necrosis accounting for 43.6% of its volume. The tumor is multicentric and has satellites, with cortical involvement detected. Proximity to the ventricular system is evident, with an ependymal contact distance of 5mm.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (GBM) in the IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation according to WHO-GBM-Necrosis.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The lesion's size and multicentricity suggest that surgical resection may be challenging.
* Focality: Given the cortical involvement, awake mapping techniques should be considered if the dominant hemisphere is affected.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Due to the tumor's proximity to eloquent areas, careful planning and consideration of intraoperative monitoring are necessary.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG_Guideline (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL): CTV expansion is required to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact.
* RTOG_Guideline (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL): Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air).
* Contraindication: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm, according to RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION.
