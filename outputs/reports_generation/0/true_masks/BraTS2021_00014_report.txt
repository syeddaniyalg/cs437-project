### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, regular-shaped, solid-enhancing tumor located in the right frontal lobe, measuring approximately 68.48 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits cortical involvement and is situated near the ventricular system, with an ependymal contact distance of 5 mm. The sphericity index suggests a relatively spherical shape. Notably, there is minimal necrosis present (0.0001 proportion). The peritumoral Flair ratio and edema-to-T2-weighted ratio indicate moderate to severe surrounding edema.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS). The frontal lobe location and cortical involvement also raise the probability of an IDH-mutant glioma (IDH-MUTANT-PRIOR).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**

* Resectability assessment: Given the lesion's size (>30mm) and cortical involvement, SRS as primary modality is contraindicated (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
* Focality: The tumor is unifocal, suggesting a single lesion.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Due to the dominant hemisphere location, awake mapping may be necessary during resection.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**

* CTV contouring modifications:
	+ Expand CTV to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) due to cortical involvement (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
