### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is WHO Grade 4 Glioblastoma, molecular subtype IDH-wildtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, heterogeneous enhancement pattern, and cortical involvement.

* Focality: Unifocal tumor with no evidence of satellites or bilateral involvement.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated due to the tumor's presence in the dominant hemisphere.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**

	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk: Monitor for potential pseudo-response due to the presence of necrosis.
* SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
