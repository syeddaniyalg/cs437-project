### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 83.89 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits heterogeneous enhancement and is characterized by significant necrosis, accounting for 33.62% of its volume. The tumor displays ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, with a peritumoral flair ratio of 1.4744 and edema-to-tumor contrast ratio of 0.1233. Focal cortical involvement is also evident.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS). The categorical radiomic profile also suggests a frontal lobe location and irregular shape, consistent with this diagnosis.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: Given the tumor's size and multifocal nature, surgical resection may be challenging.
* Focality: The lesion's multifocal pattern raises concerns about potential seeding of the contralateral hemisphere during surgery.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Due to the tumor's location in the dominant hemisphere, awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to significant necrosis and heterogeneous enhancement.
* Molecular prior: The patient's frontal predominance without bilateral/midline crossing raises IDH-mutant probability (IDH-MUTANT-PRIOR).
* Contraindication: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
