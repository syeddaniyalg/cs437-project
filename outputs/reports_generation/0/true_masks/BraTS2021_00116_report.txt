Imaging demonstrates a highly irregular, solid-enhancing tumor with a volume of approximately 170 mL, primarily located in the right frontal lobe. The lesion exhibits cortical involvement and has satellites. Proximity to the ventricular system is evident, with an ependymal contact distance of 5 mm. Necrosis accounts for approximately 6% of the tumor's volume. The heterogeneous nature of the lesion is reflected in its high entropy value (T1ce_ET_glcm_entropy: 6.45). Additionally, the peritumoral flair ratio and edema-to-tumor contrast ratio suggest significant surrounding edema.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The lesion's multifocal nature and cortical involvement suggest that surgical resection may be challenging.
* Focality: Given the bilateral nature of the tumor, eloquent cortex risks should be considered during awake mapping if the dominant hemisphere is involved.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* CTV expansion: Due to ependymal proximity/contact, a CTV expansion is required to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
* Cortical involvement: Truncate the CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) due to cortical involvement (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* SRS contraindication: Given the lesion's size (> 30mm), SRS as primary modality is contraindicated (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
