### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 84.24 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with a high peritumoral Flair ratio of 1.448 and an edema-to-T2-weighted ratio of 6.3156, indicating significant surrounding edema. The tumor has cortical involvement and is situated near the midline, with a ventricular proximity of 5 mm. Ependymal contact is present, warranting CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's size and location warrant a thorough evaluation for resectability.
* Focality: The lesion is unifocal, which may simplify surgical planning.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Due to the tumor's presence in the dominant hemisphere, awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk: The high edema-to-T2-weighted ratio and cortical involvement may indicate a potential pseudo-response to treatment. Close monitoring is recommended.
* Contraindications:
	+ SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
