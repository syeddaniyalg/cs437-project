## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, with a solid enhancing pattern and significant necrosis (6.12%). The tumor exhibits cortical involvement and has a close proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Additionally, the presence of ependymal contact warrants caution when designing radiation treatment plans.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic features and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's size and location suggest that resection may be feasible, but careful planning is required to avoid eloquent cortex risks.
* Focality: The lesion is unifocal, which favors a single surgical procedure.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**

* **RTOG Radiation Contouring Modifications:**
	+ CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* **RANO Warnings:**
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to significant necrosis and cortical involvement.
* **SRS Contraindication:** SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm, which applies in this case (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
* **Surgical Warning:** Tumor in dominant hemisphere; awake craniotomy mapping indicated to preserve speech (ELOQUENT-CORTEX-LEFT).
