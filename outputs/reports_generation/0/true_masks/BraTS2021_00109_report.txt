### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a highly irregular, ring-enhancing tumor with a volume of approximately 134 mL, predominantly located in the left frontal lobe. The lesion exhibits cortical involvement and is in close proximity to the ependymal lining, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. The presence of necrosis (19.23%) supports a diagnosis of Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is WHO Grade 4 Glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The lesion's size and location necessitate awake craniotomy mapping to preserve speech.
* Focality: Unifocal tumor, allowing for potentially curative resection.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Awake mapping indicated due to dominant hemisphere involvement.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to high heterogeneity index and cortical involvement.
* Molecular prior: IDH-mutant probability raised due to frontal predominance without bilateral/midline crossing (IDH-MUTANT-PRIOR).
* Contraindication: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
