## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a multifocal, irregularly shaped tumor with ring-enhancing pattern in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 89.19 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits cortical involvement and has satellites. Proximity to the ventricular system is evident, with an ependymal contact distance of 5 mm. Necrosis is present, accounting for 37.29% of the tumor volume. The heterogeneity index suggests moderate complexity.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's multifocal nature and cortical involvement may require a more extensive resection to achieve gross total resection.
* Focality: Due to the presence of satellites, eloquent cortex risks are present. Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech function.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to the presence of necrosis.
* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
