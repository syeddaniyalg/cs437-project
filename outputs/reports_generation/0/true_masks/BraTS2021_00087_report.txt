Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (IDH-wildtype) in accordance with WHO CNS5 criteria. The presence of necrosis supports this diagnosis, as stated by the WHO guideline "WHO-GBM-NECROSIS". This molecular subtype is also consistent with the radiomic features and clinical presentation.
