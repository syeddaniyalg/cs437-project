### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe with solid enhancing features and significant necrosis (proportion_necrosis: 0.097). The lesion exhibits ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Cortical involvement is also detected, prompting cortical truncation at the inner table of the skull. The tumor's shape and enhancement pattern are consistent with a WHO Grade 4 glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a WHO Grade 4 glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype, with molecular subtype IDH-mutant. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, ependymal contact, and cortical involvement, as well as the frontal lobe predominance without bilateral or midline crossing, which raises the probability of an IDH-mutant glioma.
