### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a highly irregular, solid-enhancing tumor with a large volume (>27 mL) located in the left frontal lobe. The tumor exhibits cortical involvement and has a maximum 3D diameter of approximately 88 mm. Proximity to the ventricular system is evident, with an ependymal contact distance of 5 mm. The presence of necrosis (14%) supports a diagnosis of Grade 4 glioblastoma in the IDH-wildtype pathway.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic and topographical findings, as well as the triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is WHO Grade 4 Glioblastoma, IDH-wildtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's multifocal nature and large volume (>27 mL) suggest that resection may be challenging.
* Focality: Given the cortical involvement, awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech function (ELOQUENT-CORTEX-LEFT).

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to the presence of necrosis.
* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
