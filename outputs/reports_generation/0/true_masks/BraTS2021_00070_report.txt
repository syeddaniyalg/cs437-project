## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 95mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits a solid enhancing pattern with heterogeneous contrast uptake and a high degree of heterogeneity. The presence of necrosis is evident, accounting for approximately 8.87% of the tumor volume. The tumor shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Cortical involvement is also detected, warranting cortical truncation at the inner table of the skull.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's size and location necessitate a multidisciplinary approach involving neurosurgery, radiation oncology, and medical oncology.
* Focality: The multifocal nature of the lesion requires careful consideration for potential satellite lesions.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG_Guideline (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL): CTV expansion is required to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact.
* RTOG_Guideline (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL): Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air).
* Contraindication: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
* Surgical Warning: Tumor in dominant hemisphere; awake craniotomy mapping indicated to preserve speech (ELOQUENT-CORTEX-LEFT).
