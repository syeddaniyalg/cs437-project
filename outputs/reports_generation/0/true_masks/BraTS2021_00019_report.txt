This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation according to WHO CNS5 criteria.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

* Focality: The lesion is unifocal, which favors surgical intervention.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Given the cortical involvement, awake mapping techniques should be considered to minimize risks of motor or language deficits.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk: The presence of necrosis may lead to a false impression of treatment response, necessitating careful monitoring and follow-up imaging.
