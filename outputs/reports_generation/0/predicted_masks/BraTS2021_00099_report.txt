### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe with solid enhancing components and significant necrosis (22.2%). Cortical involvement is also detected, prompting cortical truncation at the inner table of the skull. The tumor's volume is very large (>99 mL), with a heterogeneous index indicating significant heterogeneity.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic and topographical findings, as well as the triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (IDH-wildtype) in accordance with WHO CNS5 criteria. The presence of necrosis supports this diagnosis, as stated by the WHO Standard guideline "WHO-GBM-NECROSIS". This molecular subtype is also consistent with the IDH-wildtype pathway.
