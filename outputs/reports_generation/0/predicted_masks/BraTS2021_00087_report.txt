### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe, measuring approximately 104.26 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with no significant peritumoral edema or mass effect on surrounding brain structures. Notably, the tumor shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, which warrants CTV expansion for radiation planning. Additionally, cortical involvement is detected, necessitating truncation of the CTV margin at the inner table of the skull.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).
