### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 159mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with heterogeneous contrast uptake. Notably, it shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, which necessitates CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Based on the radiomic and topographical findings, as well as the triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).
