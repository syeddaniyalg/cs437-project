### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 102.34 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with no significant peritumoral edema or vasogenic edema. The tumor has a high degree of heterogeneity, with an entropy value of 5.3584, indicating a complex and disorganized internal structure. Notably, the lesion shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, which warrants CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic features and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

* Resectability: The tumor's size and location suggest that resection may be feasible, but careful consideration of eloquent cortex risks is necessary.
* Focality: The lesion is multicentric, which may impact the feasibility of gross total resection. Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech function.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to high entropy and heterogeneity indices.
* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
