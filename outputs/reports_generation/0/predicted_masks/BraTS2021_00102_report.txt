Imaging demonstrates a right-sided, irregularly shaped glioblastoma with heterogeneous enhancement pattern and moderate volume category. The tumor exhibits ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, measuring approximately 5mm from the midline. Cortical involvement is noted, with satellites present. The sphericity index suggests an irregular shape, while the proportion of necrosis indicates a significant component of this lesion.

This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).
