## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe with solid enhancing components and significant necrosis (18.25%). The tumor exhibits multifocal involvement and has satellites along its periphery. Proximity to the ventricular system is evident, with the closest distance being 5mm. Cortical involvement is also present, extending into the inner table of the skull. The heterogeneity index suggests a high degree of complexity in the tumor's texture.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic and topographical findings, as well as the triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's multifocal and satellite lesions may require a more extensive resection to achieve gross total resection.
* Focality: Due to the cortical involvement, awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech function (ELOQUENT-CORTEX-LEFT).

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* Radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* Contraindications:
	+ SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
