## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a highly irregular, solid-enhancing tumor with a volume of approximately 72 mL, primarily located in the left frontal lobe. The tumor exhibits ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, measuring 5 mm from the ventricular wall. Necrosis is present, accounting for 28% of the tumor's volume. Cortical involvement is detected, with satellites observed throughout the brain. The sphericity index is 0.4721, indicating a non-spherical shape.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (GBM) with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-Necrosis). The frontal lobe predominance and lack of bilateral/midline crossing raise the probability of an IDH-mutant prior (IDH-MUTANT-PRIOR).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**

* Resectability assessment: Due to the tumor's size (>30mm) and location in the dominant hemisphere, awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.
* Focality: The lesion is multicentric; surgical resection should aim to remove all visible tumor.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**

* Radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-Ependymal).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-Cortical).
* Radiation modality: Due to the tumor's size, SRS as primary modality is contraindicated (RTOG-SRS-Exclusion).
