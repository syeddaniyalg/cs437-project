## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 100.59 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with no significant peritumoral edema or vasogenic edema. The tumor is multicentric and has satellites, with cortical involvement detected. Ependymal contact is present, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (IDH-wildtype) with necrosis. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability: The tumor's size and location suggest that resection may be challenging; however, further evaluation is necessary to determine the optimal surgical approach.
* Focality: Due to the multicentric nature of the lesion, focal resection may not be feasible. Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech function, given the tumor's location in the dominant hemisphere (ELOQUENT-CORTEX-LEFT).

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* Radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* Radiation modality: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
