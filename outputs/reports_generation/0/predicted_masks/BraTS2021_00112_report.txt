Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe, measuring approximately 123.49 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with a high proportion of necrosis (25.05%). The ependymal contact and ventricular proximity (<5mm) suggest a high likelihood of infiltration into the surrounding neural structures. Additionally, cortical involvement is detected, indicating a need for careful radiation planning to avoid eloquent cortex risks.

Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).
