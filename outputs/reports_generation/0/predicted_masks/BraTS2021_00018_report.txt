## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 115mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with significant necrosis, accounting for 25.95% of its volume. The tumor shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Additionally, cortical involvement is detected, warranting truncation of the CTV margin at the inner table of the skull.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS). The frontal lobe predominance without bilateral or midline crossing raises the probability of an IDH-mutant glioma.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment is crucial due to the tumor's size and location.
* Focality is multicentric, which may impact surgical planning.
* Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech function, given the tumor's presence in the dominant hemisphere (ELOQUENT-CORTEX-LEFT).

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* CTV expansion is required to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
* Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm, given the tumor's size (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
