### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe with solid enhancing components and significant necrosis (proportion_necrosis: 0.158). The lesion exhibits ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Cortical involvement is also detected, prompting cortical truncation at the inner table of the skull. The tumor's shape and enhancement pattern are consistent with a high-grade glioma.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 IDH-wildtype glioblastoma (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS). The presence of necrosis supports this conclusion, as per WHO criteria.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: Due to the tumor's size and location, a gross total resection may not be feasible.
* Focality: Multicentric lesions require careful consideration of eloquent cortex risks. Given the dominant hemisphere involvement, awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to significant necrosis and cortical involvement.
* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
