This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation according to WHO-GBM-Necrosis. The molecular subtype is predicted to be IDH-wildtype.

* RANO warnings:

* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-Exclusion).
