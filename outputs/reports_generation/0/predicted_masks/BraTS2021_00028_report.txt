## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a highly irregular, heterogeneous tumor with moderate volume (16.006 mL) and irregular shape, primarily located in the left frontal lobe. The lesion exhibits heterogeneous enhancement patterns, with necrosis accounting for approximately 51% of its volume. The tumor displays ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Additionally, cortical involvement is detected, warranting truncation of the CTV margin at the inner table of the skull.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The lesion's size and location necessitate awake craniotomy mapping to preserve speech, as it is situated in the dominant hemisphere.
* Focality: Multicentric lesions require a comprehensive surgical approach.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to high necrosis proportion.
* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
