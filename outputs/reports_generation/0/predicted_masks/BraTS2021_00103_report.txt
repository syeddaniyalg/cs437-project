Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 108.49 mm in maximum diameter. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).
