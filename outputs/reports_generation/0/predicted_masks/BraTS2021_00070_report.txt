## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 130mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with minimal necrosis (11.4%). The tumor has ependymal contact and is situated near the ventricular system, requiring CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Cortical involvement is also detected, necessitating cortical truncation at the inner table of the skull. The lesion's shape and enhancement pattern are consistent with a high-grade glioma.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (GBM) in the IDH-wildtype pathway. The presence of necrosis supports this diagnosis, as per WHO-GBM-Necrosis guideline. Molecular analysis suggests an IDH-mutant prior, consistent with the frontal predominance and lack of bilateral/midline crossing.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The lesion's size and location require a multidisciplinary approach to determine resectability.
* Focality: Multicentricity necessitates careful planning for surgical resection.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated due to the tumor's presence in the dominant hemisphere.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall (RTOG-CTV-Ependymal).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-Cortical).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to minimal necrosis and solid enhancement.
* SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-Exclusion).
