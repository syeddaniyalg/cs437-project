### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe, measuring approximately 134.81 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with significant necrosis, accounting for 18.24% of its volume. The tumor has ependymal contact and is situated close to the ventricular system (5 mm). Additionally, it shows cortical involvement and has satellites. The volumetric analysis reveals a very large tumor volume of 122.118 mL, with a non-enhancing component accounting for 22.272 mL.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic features and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (GBM) in the IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation according to WHO-GBM-Necrosis.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's size and location suggest that resection may be challenging due to its proximity to eloquent cortex.
* Focality: The lesion is multicentric, which may impact the feasibility of gross total resection.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG_Guideline (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL): CTV expansion is required to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact.
* RTOG_Guideline (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL): Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air).
* Contraindication: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm, according to RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION.
