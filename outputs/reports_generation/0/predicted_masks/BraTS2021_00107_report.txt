## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 75.77 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits a solid enhancing pattern with significant necrosis, accounting for 14.57% of its volume. The tumor displays ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Additionally, cortical involvement is evident, warranting truncation of the CTV margin at the inner table of the skull.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's size and location suggest that resection may be feasible, but further evaluation is necessary to determine the optimal surgical approach.
* Focality: The lesion is multicentric, which may impact the feasibility of gross total resection. Eloquent cortex risks are present due to the tumor's location in the dominant hemisphere, necessitating awake craniotomy mapping to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to significant necrosis and cortical involvement.
* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
