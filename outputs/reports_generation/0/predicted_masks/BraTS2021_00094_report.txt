## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 93.9 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits a solid enhancing pattern with significant necrosis, accounting for 12.42% of its volume. The tumor shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Additionally, cortical involvement is detected, warranting truncation of the CTV margin at the inner table of the skull.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The tumor's multifocal nature and significant necrosis suggest that surgical resection may be challenging.
* Focality: Given the lesion's bilateral involvement, eloquent cortex risks are present. Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to significant necrosis.
* Contraindications: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
