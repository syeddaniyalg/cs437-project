Imaging demonstrates a multifocal, left frontal lesion with a maximum diameter of 88 mm and a volume of 27.45 mL, classified as large by volumetric criteria. The tumor's large volume, cortical involvement, and solid enhancement further support this diagnosis.

*   **Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
