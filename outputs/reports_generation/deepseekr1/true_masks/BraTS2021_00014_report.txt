## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a unifocal, right hemispheric mass located in the frontal lobe, measuring 38.358 mL (approx. 68.5mm maximum diameter). The lesion exhibits a regular shape with a sphericity index of 0.7282 and shows solid homogeneous contrast enhancement. Ependymal contact is confirmed, with minimal peritumoral edema (edema-to-tumor core ratio of 13.9894) and moderate peritumoral FLAIR signal alteration (peritumoral FLAIR ratio 1.3195). Cortical involvement is present, but no satellite lesions or significant necrosis (>0.01% volume) are identified. The lesion demonstrates unifocal behavior without midline crossing or bilateral involvement.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
The most likely diagnosis is a high-grade glioma, specifically WHO Grade 4 astrocytoma (IDH-wildtype) based on the presence of necrosis, as mandated by guideline WHO-GBM-NECROSIS (ID: WHO-GBM-NECROSIS). While a frontal predominance raises a prior for IDH-mutant status (ID: IDH-MUTANT-PRIOR), the absolute requirement for Grade 4 classification due to necrosis supersedes this molecular prior. The lesion's solid enhancement, cortical involvement, and ependymal contact are consistent with anaplastic astrocytoma or glioblastoma multiforme (IDH-wildtype).

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions
*   **Surgical Oncology:**
    *   Resection is strongly recommended given the unifocal nature and cortical involvement. Preoperative functional MRI and potentially awake mapping are advised due to frontal location and cortical involvement to preserve eloquent cortex.
    *   Gross total resection is the goal, but the ependymal contact and proximity to ventricles must be considered during planning.
    *   Histopathology and molecular testing (IDH, 1p/19q, MGMT promoter methylation) are mandatory post-resection.

*   **Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
    *   Postoperative radiation therapy is indicated. RTOG guidelines dictate specific contouring modifications: CTV expansion is required to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall due to confirmed ependymal contact (ID: RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL). Cortical involvement necessitates truncating the CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no bone/air margin) (ID: RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
    *   Chemotherapy with temozolomide is recommended concurrent with radiation, contingent on MGMT methylation status.
    *   Stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) as primary modality is contraindicated due to the lesion volume exceeding 30mL (ID: RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
    *   A RANO pseudo-response analysis should be performed periodically due to the high edema ratio (ID: RANO-EDEMA-PR).
