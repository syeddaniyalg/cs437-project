The most likely diagnosis is a high-grade glioma, specifically WHO Grade 4 astrocytoma (IDH-wildtype) based on the presence of necrosis, as mandated by WHO classification guidelines (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS). The edema-dominant phenotype raises a high pseudo-response risk per RANO criteria (RANO-PSEUDORESPONSE). While a prior raises the possibility of IDH-mutant glioma (IDH-MUTANT-PRIOR), the presence of necrosis rules out this pathway. The lesion characteristics align with a primary glioma (VASARI-PRIMARY-GLIOMA), with CNS lymphoma and metastatic disease ruled out.
