The most likely diagnosis is a high-grade glioma, specifically WHO Grade 4 astrocytoma (IDH-wildtype). This conclusion is driven by the presence of necrosis (proportion 0.0112), which, according to the triggered guideline WHO-GBM-NECROSIS, mandates a WHO Grade 4 designation within the IDH-wildtype molecular pathway. The lesion's large volume (143 mL), irregular shape, cortical involvement, and ependymal contact are consistent with the radiographic features of a Grade 4 astrocytoma.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

    *   Stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) is explicitly contraindicated as a primary treatment modality due to the lesion size exceeding 30 mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION). Standard fractionated radiotherapy is recommended.
