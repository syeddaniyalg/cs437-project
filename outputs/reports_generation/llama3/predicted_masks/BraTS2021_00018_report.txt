## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor with solid enhancing components in the left frontal lobe, measuring approximately 115mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Cortical involvement is also detected, prompting cortical truncation at the inner table of the skull. The tumor displays a high degree of heterogeneity, with a heterogeneous index of 0.6298, indicating significant variations in tissue density and intensity.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (GBM) with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. The presence of necrosis supports this diagnosis, as per WHO-GBM-Necrosis guideline. The frontal lobe predominance without bilateral or midline crossing raises the probability of an IDH-mutant prior.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**

* Resectability assessment: Due to the tumor's size and location, a comprehensive surgical planning is necessary to ensure optimal resection.
* Focality: The lesion's multicentric nature requires careful consideration of eloquent cortex risks, particularly in the dominant hemisphere. Awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**

* Radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-Ependymal).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-Cortical).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to high heterogeneity index and cortical involvement.
* Molecular prior: IDH-mutant prior is raised, which may influence treatment decisions.

Note: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-Exclusion), and awake craniotomy mapping is indicated to preserve speech in the dominant hemisphere.
