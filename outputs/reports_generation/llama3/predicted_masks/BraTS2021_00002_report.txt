## CLINICAL RADIOMICS & KNOWLEDGE GRAPH REPORT
**Patient ID:** UNKNOWN

### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a highly irregular, heterogeneous tumor with a volume of approximately 73 mL, predominantly located in the left frontal lobe. The lesion exhibits ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Cortical involvement is also detected, warranting cortical truncation at the inner table of the skull. The tumor displays a high degree of heterogeneity, with a necrosis proportion of 40.62%. Additionally, the lesion demonstrates midline crossing and has satellites.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma (GBM) in the IDH-wildtype pathway. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation according to WHO criteria.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: The lesion's size and location necessitate awake craniotomy mapping to preserve speech.
* Focality: Multicentric involvement requires careful surgical planning to ensure complete resection.
* Eloquent cortex risks: Awake mapping is indicated due to the tumor's presence in the dominant hemisphere.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG radiation contouring modifications:
	+ CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL).
	+ Cortical involvement detected; truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-CORTICAL).
* RANO warnings:
	+ Pseudo-response risk due to high heterogeneity and necrosis proportion.
* Molecular prior: IDH-mutant probability raised due to frontal predominance without bilateral/midline crossing (IDH-MUTANT-PRIOR).
* Contraindication: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-EXCLUSION).
