### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe with solid enhancing components and significant necrosis (19.7%). The lesion exhibits ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, necessitating CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall. Cortical involvement is also detected, prompting cortical truncation at the inner table of the skull. The tumor's shape and enhancement pattern are consistent with a high-grade glioma.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 IDH-wildtype glioblastoma. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-Necrosis). The molecular subtype is predicted to be IDH-wildtype due to the frontal predominance without bilateral or midline crossing, raising the probability of an IDH-mutant prior.
