### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe, measuring approximately 86.96 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with a high edema-to-tumor contrast ratio of 54.03, indicating significant surrounding edema. The tumor is found to be unifocal and has cortical involvement, with a peritumoral flair ratio of 1.82 suggesting moderate heterogeneity. Notably, the ependymal contact is confirmed, warranting CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic and topographical findings, as well as the triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-Necrosis).
