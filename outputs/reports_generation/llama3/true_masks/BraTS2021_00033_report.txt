### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe, measuring approximately 110mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing characteristics and is situated near the ependymal lining of the ventricle, with a proximity of 5mm. The tumor has a moderate degree of heterogeneity (0.6384) and shows cortical involvement. No necrosis or satellite lesions are detected.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a high-grade glioma, WHO Grade IV, with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. The presence of ependymal contact and cortical involvement supports this conclusion. Specifically, the RTOG guideline "RTOG-CTV-EPENDYMAL" recommends CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact, which is consistent with a high-grade glioma.

* Resectability assessment: The lesion's size and location suggest that surgical resection may be feasible, but further evaluation by a neurosurgeon is necessary to determine the optimal approach.
* Focality: The tumor is unifocal, which favors a single surgical procedure.
