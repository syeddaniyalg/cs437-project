### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a highly irregular, solid-enhancing tumor with a large volume (>21mL) located in the right frontal lobe, exhibiting cortical involvement and midline crossing. The tumor displays a heterogeneous intensity profile with a high entropy value (7.3245), indicating significant heterogeneity. Proximity to the ventricular system is evident, with an ependymal contact distance of 5mm. Necrosis is present, accounting for approximately 4.09% of the tumor volume.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. This conclusion is supported by the presence of necrosis, which mandates a Grade 4 designation in the IDH-wildtype pathway (WHO-GBM-NECROSIS).
