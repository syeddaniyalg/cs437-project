### 1. Findings (Radiomic & Topographical Summary)
Imaging demonstrates a large, irregularly shaped tumor located in the right frontal lobe, measuring approximately 104.47 mm in maximum diameter. The lesion exhibits solid enhancing features with a high peritumoral Flair ratio of 1.8567 and an edema-to-T2-weighted ratio of 16.1225, indicating significant surrounding edema. The tumor is found to be unifocal, with no evidence of satellites or bilateral involvement. Notably, the lesion shows ependymal contact and proximity to the ventricular system, warranting CTV expansion to include the ipsilateral ventricular wall.

### 2. Integrated Diagnosis (WHO CNS5 Framework)
Based on the radiomic profile and triggered clinical guidelines, the most likely diagnosis is a Grade 4 glioblastoma with an IDH-wildtype molecular subtype. The presence of necrosis supports this conclusion, as per WHO-GBM-Necrosis guideline.

### 3. Clinical Management & Prescriptions

**Surgical Oncology:**
* Resectability assessment: Given the lesion's size and location, a gross total resection is unlikely. A more conservative approach with subtotal resection or biopsy may be considered.
* Focality: The unifocal nature of the tumor suggests that eloquent cortex risks are low.

**Radiation/Medical Oncology:**
* RTOG-Guideline: CTV expansion required to include ipsilateral ventricular wall due to ependymal proximity/contact (RTOG-CTV-Ependymal).
* RTOG-Guideline: Cortical involvement detected. Truncate CTV margin at the inner table of the skull (no margin into bone/air) (RTOG-CTV-Cortical).
* Contraindication: SRS as primary modality is contraindicated for lesions > 30mm (RTOG-SRS-Exclusion).
